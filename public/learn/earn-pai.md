## What Earn PAI is

Earn PAI is a teaching mode. When it is on, DSI listens for knowledge you can give it, checks it, and pays you in PAI, the network's reward token, when that knowledge is new and true.

It is off until you turn it on. You can turn it off at any time with the same button.

## What earns PAI

You earn by teaching DSI things it does not know yet, and that other people need to know.

The best teaching comes from your own experience: how something is really done in your job, a rule that changed recently, a local fact that is not written down well, or a mistake you notice DSI making.

Corrections are valuable. If DSI gets something wrong, tell it what is right and why.

Things that can be checked pay fastest. A calculation, a number with its source, or a step-by-step procedure that works can be verified straight away.

## What does not earn PAI

Junk does not earn anything. Neither does knowledge DSI already has, or something many people have already taught it. Only the first person to teach a piece of knowledge is paid for it.

Opinions, guesses and small talk do not earn PAI. Copied text does not either.

Personal details are never paid and never shared. Names, contact details and anything that could identify you or someone else are removed or kept private on your device.

## How your knowledge is judged

Every piece of knowledge goes through the Learning Fabric, which checks five things.

Accuracy. Is it true? A calculator checks numbers at once. Other facts need evidence from independent validators before they count as verified.

Novelty. Is it new? It is compared with everything the network already knows. A repeat is rejected.

Model gap. Does DSI actually need it? Knowledge that fills a real gap in what the models can do is worth more.

Provenance. Where does it come from? Your own experience and a good track record raise your score.

Privacy. Personal details are stripped out before anything leaves your device, and anything still too personal is blocked.

## How you are paid

Each verified piece of knowledge earns up to one PAI. The amount is the product of its accuracy, novelty, model gap and provenance scores, so true, new and genuinely useful knowledge earns the most.

When something can be checked straight away, you hear a reward sound and see the PAI arrive. When it needs validators, it is saved as pending and pays once they confirm it.

You can teach a limited number of things per period. The limit grows as more of your teaching is verified.

Your PAI balance, what you have earned and what is still pending are always in Credits and wallet.

## How to start

Just talk. Tell DSI something from your experience that it probably gets wrong, or correct it when it makes a mistake.

DSI will suggest areas where your knowledge is worth the most. You can also ask it: what should I teach you?
