Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated 28 September 2026. Questions: yousef@clanz.com.

What we collect, and why

  • Sign-up forms (“Register your interest”, “Become a node”): your email, and your name, roles and notes if you give them, plus your approximate country, which our hosting provider derives from your connection. We use them only to write to you about the project.
  • Accounts in the app: the email or wallet address you sign in with, your organisation, members and settings, and API keys (we store a hash, not the key).
  • Your own vendor keys (Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini and others): stored encrypted, and used only to call that vendor for you.
  • Usage records for every API request: time, model, provider, token counts, cost, latency, status and a hash of the request and response. These records do not contain the text of your prompts or answers.
  • Files and batches you upload through the API are stored until you delete them, so the service can process them.
  • Node operators: your node's name, public URL, models, approximate location (unless you set it to hidden) and the work it serves, which is shown on the public live map and credited as PAI reward points.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell personal data, and we don't use it for advertising.
  • This site uses no analytics or tracking scripts and sets no tracking cookies.
  • Chat history in the app is kept in your browser, not on our servers.

Who else sees your data

  • The provider that answers a request (a model vendor you chose, or a network node) receives the text of that request in order to answer it. Network nodes don't receive your account identity. Use DSI Synapse on your device to strip identifying details before a request leaves it.
  • Cloudflare hosts the site, the API and the database.
  • Privy handles sign-in and creates your self-custodial wallet.
  • Payment processors, when top-ups open, receive what they need to process a payment.

How long we keep it, and your rights

We keep sign-up details until you ask us to delete them, and account data while your account exists. Email yousef@clanz.com to see, correct, export or delete your data, or to object to how we use it; we answer within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version, and material changes will be announced on this site.

Decentralised.si is run by its founders. The legal entity that operates it will be named on this page before any token launch.
